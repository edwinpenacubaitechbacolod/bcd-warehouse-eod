How it works

  1. Fill out your report using your phone.
  2. Tap Review my report and check the details.
  3. Enter the team code and tap Submit EOD report by email.
  4. Check for the successful email confirmation in the newly opened tab. Viber sharing remains available as a backup.
Privacy: Drafts stay in this phone's browser. On email submission, the report is sent to an authorized Google Apps Script to be emailed to the Warehouse In-Charge; the updated companion script stores submissions in a private Google Sheet. Avoid including confidential customer details.
